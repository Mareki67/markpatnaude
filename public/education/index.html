Speaking & Education

Practical learning grounded in real-world experience.

Technology, AI and cybersecurity become more useful when people can connect them to decisions they actually need to make.

My workshops focus on practical application: understanding the situation, examining the available evidence, and giving participants approaches they can continue using after the session ends.

AI & Better Decisions

Understand the market before creating for it.

These workshops use AI as a tool for discovery, analysis and decision-making rather than simply as a content-generation engine.

Career Market Discovery

Read Your Job Market

Using AI to Make Better Career Decisions

Most job seekers begin with a single posting and immediately start creating: rewriting a résumé, generating a cover letter or preparing an application. This workshop starts earlier.

Participants use AI to investigate a body of current job postings, identify recurring requirements and market patterns, and compare those findings with their own experience before deciding where and how to compete.

Key idea: Creation follows understanding.

Small Business Market Discovery

Before You Start the Business

Using AI to Test the Market

A business idea often moves quickly from inspiration to creation: a name, logo, website, business plan and marketing. This workshop puts discovery first.

Participants use AI and publicly available information to investigate customers, competitors, offerings, demand signals and potential differentiation before making significant investments of time or money.

The objective is not to have AI decide whether a business will succeed. It is to make the decision with better information and identify what still requires direct human validation.

Key idea: Understand the market before creating for the market.

Cybersecurity & Small Business

Make technology risk practical.

Cybersecurity can become abstract very quickly. These sessions focus on recognizable risks, practical decisions and the actions small organizations and employees can actually take.

Cybersecurity Awareness

Cybersecurity Starts With People

Practical Cybersecurity for Small Businesses and Employees

Many of the risks small organizations encounter begin with ordinary decisions made by ordinary people.

This workshop makes cybersecurity practical through recognizable situations involving phishing, passwords and multi-factor authentication, guest Wi-Fi, devices, sensitive information, vendors, backups and what employees should do when something does not look right.

The goal is not to turn employees into cybersecurity specialists. It is to help people recognize risk, understand their role and make safer decisions.

Key idea: Better security begins with better everyday decisions.

Focused Small Business Session

Is Your Guest Wi-Fi Putting Your Business at Risk?

A practical look at one commonly overlooked area of small-business cybersecurity

Guest Wi-Fi is convenient for customers, visitors and employees, but poor configuration or weak separation can create unnecessary business risk.

This session uses guest Wi-Fi as a practical entry point into network separation, access controls, employee and personal devices, passwords, routers and access points, sensitive business systems, and the questions business owners should ask whoever manages their technology.

Key idea: Convenience should not create unnecessary access to the business.

A Common Approach

Understand before you act.

Across career decisions, business ideas and technology risk, the subjects may be different but the underlying discipline is similar: define the decision, discover relevant evidence, analyze what it suggests, validate assumptions, compare options and then decide what to do next.

Define → Discover → Analyze → Validate → Compare → Decide → Act

AI can accelerate discovery and analysis and identify patterns people might otherwise miss. It can also misunderstand evidence, make assumptions and produce confident answers that are not supported by the underlying information.

AI identifies patterns. Human judgment determines what those patterns mean.
