Leadership

Technology creates capability. Leadership turns that capability into organizational results.

My approach to technology leadership has consistently come back to three ideas: understand the larger organizational objective, work across boundaries to create shared ownership, and focus on practical solutions that produce meaningful results.

Strategic

See the whole organization.

Align technology with business goals.

Understand the problem, evaluate risk and investment in context, and build capability for where the organization is going.

Collaborative

Knock down silos.

Build shared ownership around organizational goals.

Create open communication across business leaders, employees, technology teams and strategic partners so individual decisions contribute to the whole.

Pragmatic

Make it work in practice.

Focus on practical solutions that deliver results.

Modernize when modernization creates value. Simplify when simplicity creates strength. Measure the decision by what improves for the organization.

About

Technology. Business. People.

I have spent my career working at the intersection of technology, business operations and people.

My experience spans enterprise infrastructure, IT operations, cybersecurity governance, service management, enterprise applications, cloud platforms and technology transformation. The common thread has always been the same: technology should create capability and value rather than become an end in itself.

Today, that work also includes writing, education and research into how emerging technologies — particularly artificial intelligence — are reshaping organizations, careers and the decisions people make.
